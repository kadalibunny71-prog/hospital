CAREPOINT HOSPITAL & HEALTHCARE WEBSITE
Files: index.html (page structure), style.css (responsive design), script.js (navigation and appointment form), README.txt (instructions).

How to run:
1. Extract the ZIP.
2. Keep all files in the same folder.
3. Open index.html in Chrome or Edge.

Important:
- CarePoint is a fictional demo. Replace sample hospital details, address, doctors, timings and services with verified information.
- Appointment form is front-end only. It validates and displays a message; it does not send, store or confirm appointments. A real site needs a secure backend and scheduling integration.
- For emergencies in India, call 112 or go to the nearest emergency department. Do not use this demo form for urgent medical needs.
- Health information is general education, not diagnosis or personal medical advice.
- Images and fonts require an internet connection.
